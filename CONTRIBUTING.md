Not accepted. The licence grants no right to modify or distribute the code, so
there is no basis on which to take a change in — and accepting outside code
would muddy the ownership of what is a commercial product. Pull requests are
closed without review.

## Issues

Welcome, for:

- bugs on the site
- broken links
- typos or factual errors in a lesson
- questions about how something here works

Please include the URL, what you expected, what happened instead, and your
browser and OS. Issues with the CLI belong in
[luxctl](https://github.com/projectlighthouse-io/luxctl).

## Security

Do **not** open a public issue. Email thearyanahmed@projectlighthouse.io with what you
found, the URL or endpoint it affects, the steps to reproduce it, and what an
attacker could do with it. Every report is acknowledged.
